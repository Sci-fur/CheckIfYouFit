# Data-source notes

The scholarship directory is a small starter list, not a comprehensive or automatically updated feed. It intentionally avoids hard-coded deadlines because application cycles and country eligibility can change.

Official provider links used in `lib/data.js` include:

- Chevening: https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/
- Commonwealth Scholarship Commission: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/Scholarships
- Australia Awards: https://www.dfat.gov.au/people-to-people/australia-awards
- Australia Awards Bangladesh intake information: https://www.dfat.gov.au/people-to-people/australia-awards/participating-countries/bangladesh-information-for-intake
- Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters: https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters
- DAAD Scholarship Database: https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/
- MEXT Scholarship: https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/planning/scholarships/mext-scholarships/
- Global Korea Scholarship: https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/swb/plan/scholarship.do
- Fulbright Foreign Student Program: https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/

Country links in the assessment and comparison tools are official study, education or immigration starting points where available. A country-level link does not replace the exact course page or official visa instructions relevant to an individual applicant.

Before launch, review every link, add a `lastVerified` date, confirm that each opportunity is available to applicants from Bangladesh, and assign an owner for ongoing updates.
